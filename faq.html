Ignition & plugs

Do iridium plugs always last longer? Usually — but turbo, oil consumption, and poor fuel quality can shorten any electrode.

Can I gap iridium plugs at home? Prefer OEM gap; if adjustment is required, use a proper gapping tool — avoid prying on the center electrode.
