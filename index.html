Technical Advisory for Ghana

The Real Cost of Fake Spark Plugs in Accra

Upwards of 70% of "Iridium" plugs sold in local markets are basic copper plugs with printed counterfeit brand labels. Their incorrect internal resistance destroys expensive ignition coils and damages ECUs.

Burnt Ignition Coils & Drivers

Imitation plugs lack calibrated internal ceramic resistors, causing electrical flash-back that fries your vehicle's engine computer.

Electrode Shearing & Cylinder Damage

Counterfeit tips are glued rather than 360° laser welded. Under high combustion heat, tips detach directly into engine combustion chambers.
