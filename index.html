Consumer Alert in Ghana

Why 8 out of 10 "Iridium" plugs sold locally fail.

Accra and Kumasi spare-part markets are flooded with imitation spark plugs. Counterfeiters use cheap, standard copper cores disguised with spray paint and stamped with Denso, NGK, or Mobis logos.

Blown Ignition Coils & ECUs

Fake plugs feature incorrect internal resistance, sending voltage spikes directly into your vehicle’s engine computer.

Center Electrode Melt-Off

Counterfeits lack true Iridium alloys. The ceramic and ground electrode melt under load, dropping metal shards into your cylinders.
