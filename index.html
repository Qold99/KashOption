No cash refunds

All sales are final. We do not offer cash refunds. Exchanges are only available for factory defects and must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. After this window, no exchange or refund will be processed.

Fitment responsibility

We supply genuine parts as described. Misdiagnosis by the customer's technician or incorrect fitment is not our liability. Parts cannot be returned once installed or used. We recommend your technician confirms compatibility before installation.

Electrical warranty

We are not liable for device damage caused by faulty vehicle alternators, incorrect voltage, or external power surges. Customers are responsible for ensuring their vehicle's electrical system is in good condition before connecting any purchased devices.

Logistics

Return shipping costs are the sole responsibility of the buyer. If an exchange is approved under our factory-defect policy, you must cover the cost of sending the item back. We are not responsible for lost or damaged return shipments unless we provide a prepaid label.
