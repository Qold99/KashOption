Fitment policy

OEM-first, evidence-backed, and transparent — so your bay can stand behind every install with clear documentation.

Principles

  • We prioritize manufacturer-spec plugs for the stated engine and market variant.
  • When multiple crosses exist, we disclose resistor type, reach, and heat range implications.
  • We do not recommend “universal” plugs for modern coil-on-plat or DI engines without verification.

If something is unclear

Use the OEM plug finder with photos of the current plug stamping and engine bay label. For policy questions tied to an active order, reference order tracking.

Fitment dispute? Send order ID + photos — we escalate with dispatch context.
